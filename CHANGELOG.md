# Changelog — Hungry Birds POS

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## [2026-11-26] Brand Logo & Favicon Update (Slice 28)
- **Tab Favicon, PWA Icons & Vector Assets (`index.html`, `public/manifest.json`, `public/sw.js`, `public/*.png`, `public/*.svg`):** Replaced the placeholder orange bird SVG with the official full-color Hungry Birds chef-rooster emblem across `favicon-32x32.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `logo.png`, `logo.svg`, and `favicon.svg`, and bumped service worker cache to `hb-pos-modern-v4.2`.
- **POS UI Brand Placements (`src/App.tsx`, `src/components/LoginScreen.tsx`, `src/components/Sidebar.tsx`):** Updated the top header bar (`App.tsx`), sign-in card & Owner setup modal (`LoginScreen.tsx`), and navigation sidebar brand card (`Sidebar.tsx`) to display `/logo.png` cleanly centered with crisp transparent edges.

## [2026-11-26] Weight-Based (Per kg) Menu Item Pricing (Slice 27)
- **Menu Setup (`src/lib/db/types.ts`, `src/lib/menu.ts`, `src/components/AdminSettingsModal.tsx`):** Added `MenuItem.soldByWeight` toggle so weighted dishes (e.g. Grilled Fish at `Rs 1,800/kg`) store per-kg rates and configure ingredient deductions per 1 kg sold.
- **Weight Picker & Cart UX (`src/components/WeightModal.tsx`, `src/components/MenuGrid.tsx`, `src/components/CartPanel.tsx`, `src/components/ModifierModal.tsx`, `src/lib/cart.ts`, `src/App.tsx`):** Added decimal- and comma-aware `kg`/`g` weight picker modal with presets (`0.5`, `1`, `1.5`, `2 kg`), locked per-kg rate, whole-rupee line rounding (`computeWeightedPrice`), flat per-fish extras preservation, separate line identity per weighed portion (`2.4 kg` vs `1.8 kg`), and tappable cart weight badge to re-weigh items.
- **Inventory, Shift COGS & Refunds (`src/lib/sales.ts`, `src/lib/shift.ts`, `src/lib/refunds.ts`, `src/components/RefundModal.tsx`):** Scaled recipe stock verification, deductions, restorations, and live shift COGS by sold `weightKg` (`recipeMultiplierForLine`) while keeping modifiers flat per fish.
- **Receipts, KOT & KDS (`src/lib/receipt.ts`, `src/components/KitchenDisplayModal.tsx`, `src/components/HeldOrdersModal.tsx`, `src/components/SalesHistoryModal.tsx`):** Formatted weight × per-kg rate cleanly across 80mm/58mm ESC/POS, HTML print receipts, KOT (`2.4kg Grilled Fish`), KDS cards, Held Orders, and Sales History while keeping `Total Qty` unit count accurate.

## [2026-11-24] Security Hardening & Financial/Workflow Bug Fixes

### Security (`SEC-01`..`SEC-06`)
- **Firestore Rules (`firestore.rules`):** Restricted `/onlineOrders` `get` to owning customers or active staff and validated customer `create` payloads (`status == 'PENDING'`, non-empty `items`, `total >= 0`); locked `/coupons` updates to one-way single-use loyalty redemption or monotonic global `usedCount` increments; split staff permissions into `isAdmin`, `isManagerOrOwner`, `isCashierOrAbove`, and `isStaff` with `active != false`, locking `setting_ownerPinHash` writes and `/auditLogs` deletes to Owners. *(Publish `firestore.rules` to Firebase Console for live enforcement.)*
- **Auth & Sessions (`src/lib/auth.ts`, `src/App.tsx`, `src/lib/staffAccounts.ts`):** Deferred persisting `hb.syncToken` until after staff role and active checks pass (clearing stale tokens on rejection); added `verifyStoredSession()` on boot to detect tampered `sessionStorage` roles or locally disabled accounts; added Identity Toolkit `accounts:delete` rollback if staff Firestore profile provisioning fails.
- **Backup & Restore (`src/lib/backup.ts`):** Redacted `ownerPinHash`, `sync.*` settings, and `users.passwordHash` from `collectBackup()`; filtered protected setting keys and normalized malformed row shapes in `importBackup()`.
- **XSS & CSP (`export-data.html`, `vercel.json`, `netlify.toml`):** Replaced `innerHTML` store rendering in `export-data.html` with DOM `textContent` nodes; removed `'unsafe-eval'`, `https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com`, and `https://unpkg.com` from `script-src`.

### Financial, Inventory, CRM & Workflow Fixes (`BUG-017`..`BUG-020`)
- **Shift Drawer & Refunds (`src/lib/shift.ts`, `src/lib/refunds.ts`, `src/components/RefundModal.tsx`):** Allocated refunds across `Cash`, `Card`, and `Online` tenders (with proportional fallback for legacy refunds) and deducted them from `netCash`, `expectedDrawerCash`, `cardCollected`, and `onlineCollected`; added a `Refund via` selector; fixed `paidRatio()` to discount item revenue accurately on discounted delivery orders and return `0` on `Rs 0` comped orders.
- **Inventory & Customer CRM Integrity (`src/lib/sales.ts`, `src/lib/customers.ts`, `src/components/SalesHistoryModal.tsx`, `src/App.tsx`):** Updated `restoreInventory()` and sale re-open (`onReopen`) to count only unrefunded (`qty - refundedQty`) units and modifiers; added `adjustCustomerForRefund()` and `reverseCustomerSale()` so customer `totalOrders`, `totalSpent`, and `loyaltyPoints` stay exact across refunds, edits, and voids.
- **Online Orders, Logout, Opening Cash & Payroll (`src/lib/onlineOrders.ts`, `src/lib/held.ts`, `src/lib/staff.ts`, `src/components/Sidebar.tsx`, `src/components/OpeningCashModal.tsx`):** Preserved online order delivery fees/discounts (`deriveOnlineOrderCharges`) and held-order links (`onlineOrderId`/`onlineOrderCloudId`); awaited `closeOnlineOrder()` with error handling; switched logout handlers to full `resetOrder()`; enabled `Opening cash` for Managers/Cashiers when starting a fresh float (PIN-protected when overriding `> 0`); guarded `paySalary()` against duplicate monthly payouts.
- **UI & Filter Polish (`src/components/Sidebar.tsx`, `src/components/ExpensesModal.tsx`, `src/components/SuppliersModal.tsx`, `src/components/StaffModal.tsx`):** Synced Sidebar KDS badge with active kitchen tickets; aligned Expenses `"This week"` to Monday and `isApproved()`; replaced native `window.confirm()` delete prompts with inline `Confirm`/`Cancel` buttons.
